# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and is incorporated here by
reference; the Covenant is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

In short, we pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, and we expect
everyone in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) to:

- be welcoming, respectful and patient, and give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
- focus on what is best for the project and its community;
- take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal or political attacks, trolling, publishing
others' private information, and anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in
a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not
follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they
deem inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private
[vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/aomarai/Aha/security/advisories/new) form and say it is a
conduct report, or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
promptly and confidentially.
